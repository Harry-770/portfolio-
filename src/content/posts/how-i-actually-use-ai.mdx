I use AI tools every day. I also delete a lot of what they write. Both things are true.

### Where it's genuinely great

- Boilerplate I've written a hundred times: forms, API wrappers, test scaffolding.
- "Explain this regex to me like I'm tired." (I am always tired.)
- First drafts of things I'll heavily edit, like this site's video renderer.

### Where I don't trust it

Architecture decisions, anything security-related, and "make it look good". It will happily give you something that looks like every other site on the internet. Taste is still the job.

My rule: if I can't explain a line of code in a review, it doesn't go in, no matter who or what wrote it.

> It's a very fast junior dev who never gets bored. Review it like one.
