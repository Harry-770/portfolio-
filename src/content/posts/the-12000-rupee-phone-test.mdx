My laptop is fast. My office Wi-Fi is fast. Most of the people using the sites I build have neither.

So a couple of years ago I bought a budget Android phone, the kind that's on sale every other week, and made a rule: nothing ships until it feels okay on that phone, on mobile data.

### What it caught

- A hero video that was 14 MB. On 4G it simply never played.
- A font setup that blocked rendering for almost 3 seconds.
- A "smooth scroll" library that made scrolling feel like wading through dal.

### What I do now

I set a budget before I start: roughly 200 KB of JS, first content in under 2 seconds on 4G, and no layout jumps. If a feature doesn't fit, it doesn't ship, or it gets lazy-loaded.

> DevTools throttling is good. An actual cheap phone in your hand is better.

It's ₹12,000 well spent. I've spent more than that on keyboards I don't use.
