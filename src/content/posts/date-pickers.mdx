On the clinic booking project we tested the old flow with real receptionists. The date picker alone took one of them 40 seconds. She'd been using it every day for two years.

What we ended up with: the next 14 days as big tappable chips, plus a small "pick another date" link for the rare case. Bookings went up. Nobody missed the calendar grid.

Most "pick a date" problems are really "pick one of the next few days" problems. Design for that, and keep the calendar as the fallback.
